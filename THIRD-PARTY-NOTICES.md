# Third-party notices

Weald itself is licensed under [AGPL-3.0-or-later](./LICENSE) with the
[section 7(b) attribution requirement](./ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md). The components
below are redistributed inside the built bundle and keep their own licences.

The list is derived from the `sources` recorded in `dist/**/*.js.map` — i.e. what
is actually in the shipped JavaScript — not from `npm ls`. That distinction
matters here: the dependency tree reports over 250 packages, because the shared
engine declares an optional signalling stack it does not use by default. **Three**
of them reach the browser.

| Component | Version | Licence | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|---|
| [@ben-gy/game-engine](https://github.com/ben-gy/gh-game-engine) | 1.3.2 | MIT | Netcode, rounds, lobby, join QR, RNG, sound, storage, mobile hardening. |
| [trystero](https://github.com/dmotz/trystero) | 0.21.8 | MIT | WebRTC peer-to-peer rooms over public signalling relays. Arrives through the engine, pinned exactly; never a direct dependency here. |
| [@noble/secp256k1](https://github.com/paulmillr/noble-secp256k1) | 3.1.0 | MIT | Elliptic-curve signatures, used by Trystero's Nostr signalling strategy. |

No fonts, icons, images or audio are third-party: every glyph is a Unicode
character rendered in the system font, the icons are generated by
`scripts/gen-icons.mjs`, and all sound is synthesised at runtime by the engine's
Web Audio module. There are no asset files to attribute.

Build-time only, and therefore not redistributed: Vite, TypeScript, Vitest, jsdom.

The served copy of this file is `public/third-party-notices.txt`.
